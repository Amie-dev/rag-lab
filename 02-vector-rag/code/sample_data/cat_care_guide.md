# Domestic Cat Care and Feline Health Guide

## Feline Nutrition and Grooming Instructions
Proper nutrition is essential for domestic cats. Feed high-quality commercial cat food appropriate for your cat's life stage (kitten, adult, senior). Ensure continuous access to clean, fresh water. Daily grooming prevents hairballs and keeps the cat's coat healthy.

## Cat Vaccination and Health Recommendations
Routine veterinary checkups, core vaccinations (such as rabies and FVRCP), and preventative treatments for fleas and ticks are crucial for indoor and outdoor feline wellbeing. Watch for signs of illness like lethargy, changes in appetite, or vocalization changes.

## Indoor Environmental Enrichment
Cats thrive in safe indoor spaces equipped with scratching posts, perches, interactive toys, and clean litter boxes cleaned daily.
