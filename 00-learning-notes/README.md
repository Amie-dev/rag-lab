## 🗺️ The 8-Phase Learning Roadmap

```text
PHASE 1 — RAG Fundamentals & Basic Architecture
   │      ├── What is RAG? Why RAG vs Fine-tuning?
   │      └── Baseline Naive RAG Pipeline
   ▼
PHASE 2 — Indexing, Chunking & Vector Databases
   │      ├── Document Loading & Chunking Strategies
   │      └── Embeddings & Vector Similarity Search
   ▼
PHASE 3 — Hybrid Search, Metadata Filtering & Reranking
   │      ├── BM25 Keyword Search + Vector Dense Fusion (RRF)
   │      └── Cross-Encoder Reranking & Structured Filtering
   ▼
PHASE 4 — Query Transformation & Multi-Level Chunking
   │      ├── Multi-Query, Query Expansion & HyDE
   │      └── Parent-Document & Hierarchical Retrieval
   ▼
PHASE 5 — Advanced Contextual & Multi-Hop RAG
   │      ├── Multi-Hop Iterative Reasoning
   │      └── Conversational History & Contextual Chunking
   ▼
PHASE 6 — Knowledge Graphs & GraphRAG
   │      ├── Neo4j, Cypher & Entity-Relation Extraction
   │      └── Vector + Knowledge Graph Hybrid Systems
   ▼
PHASE 7 — Agentic, Self-Reflective & Dynamic RAG
   │      ├── Agent-controlled Retrieval Tools
   │      └── Corrective RAG (CRAG), Self-RAG & Adaptive RAG
   ▼
PHASE 8 — Production RAG, Memory, Multimodal & Evaluation
          ├── Memory Stores, Multimodal Inputs (Text/Images/Tables)
          └── Ragas, TruLens, Observability & Security
```
