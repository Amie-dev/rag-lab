# Statutory & Legal Compliance Reference Manual

## Section 420 IPC Compliance
- **Overview**: Deals with cheating and dishonestly inducing delivery of property under statutory criminal codes.
- **Application**: Mandatory disclosure requirements for financial platforms operating across regional jurisdictions.

## GDPR Article 17 - Right to Erasure
- **Overview**: Data subjects have the statutory right to obtain from the data controller the erasure of personal data concerning them without undue delay.
- **Implementation**: Automated data scrubbing across inverted indexes, vector databases, and log archives upon receipt of validated request.
